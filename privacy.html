Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Client-Side & Privacy First Architecture

We respect your privacy. This application is designed to operate locally in your browser with a zero-tracking philosophy. We do not sell, monetize, or transmit any personal identifiable information (PII) to remote servers or cloud databases.

2. Geolocation Data

Location data is accessed only when you explicitly click a location button. Your device’s GPS coordinates are processed exclusively within your browser’s local JavaScript runtime to compute nearby stop distances and journey calculations. Your coordinates are never transmitted over the internet, stored in external databases, or shared with third parties.

3. Local Storage

This site uses your browser’s native localStorage solely to remember your UI preference (such as light or dark mode). This data resides exclusively on your local device and can be erased at any time by clearing your browser site data.

4. Hosting & Third-Party Services

This site is hosted via GitHub, which may collect basic server log data (such as visitor IP addresses) strictly for network security and infrastructure maintenance in accordance with GitHub's Privacy Statement. We also use Abacus Counter to maintain a aggregate page view count; this service counts visits without building user profiles or storing tracking cookies.
